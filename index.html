    Privacy Notice

    This is a plain description of what Loominous actually does with your data, not a legal document.

    What's stored: your dreams, their readings, any personal meaning you've added to a symbol or theme, any relevant/not-relevant feedback you've given on a Discovery, anything you add to your profile, and your login (username, email, and a password that's hashed -- never kept as plain text, and never visible to anyone, including whoever runs this app).

    Who sees it: only you. Every dream, reading, and profile is tied to your account and kept separate from everyone else's.

    Where it goes: your dream text is sent to Anthropic (Claude) to generate AI interpretations and related responses. If you've added information to your profile, relevant details such as your name, age, gender, relationship status, birthplace, current location, or biography may also be included to personalize interpretations, follow-up conversations, and Insights.

    Your dream text is also processed by Voyage AI to create numerical embeddings that help identify similarities and connections between dreams. These embeddings are stored in Pinecone together with identifiers used to associate them with your account and dreams. According to the application's current implementation, the original dream text and profile information are not stored in Pinecone.

    When you delete a dream or your account, Loominous requests deletion of the associated vector records from Pinecone. Processing, retention, and deletion by external service providers may also be subject to their respective policies.

    Email: used only to verify your account and send a password-reset link if you ask for one. Nothing else is ever sent to it.

    Exporting your dreams (Settings → Account → Download my dreams) gives you a plain text file of every dream you've recorded, with its symbols, emotions, themes, reading, and any follow-up conversation -- built entirely on your own device from what's already loaded, so nothing is sent anywhere to generate it. It doesn't include your profile details (name, bio, etc.) or any personal meanings you've added to symbols.

    Deleting your account (Settings → Account → Delete my account) removes your dreams, personal meanings, Discovery feedback, profile, and login immediately and permanently -- there's no recovery after that.
