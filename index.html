Privacy Notice

This is a plain description of what Loominous actually does with your data, not a legal document.

What's stored: your dreams, their readings, anything you add to your profile, and your login (username, email, and a password that's hashed -- never kept as plain text, and never visible to anyone, including whoever runs this app).

Who sees it: only you. Every dream, reading, and profile is tied to your account and kept separate from everyone else's.

Where it goes: your dream's text is sent to Anthropic (Claude) and Voyage AI to generate its reading and let you search across your own archive -- never to anyone else, and never used to advertise to you.

Email: used only to verify your account and send a password-reset link if you ask for one. Nothing else is ever sent to it.

Exporting your dreams (Settings → Account → Download my dreams) gives you a plain text file of everything you've logged, built entirely on your own device -- nothing is sent anywhere to generate it.

Deleting your account (Settings → Account → Delete my account) removes your dreams, profile, and login immediately and permanently -- there's no recovery after that.
