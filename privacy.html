Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Loominous is a private dream journal. Your dreams are personal, so this page explains in plain language what we collect, who processes it, and how you can remove it.

What we store

  • Your account: a username, an optional email address, and a password stored only as a salted hash. We cannot see your password.
  • Your journal: the dreams you write, the AI-generated readings, symbols, emotions, people, places and themes taken from them, any personal meanings you add, your feedback on Discovery suggestions, and follow-up conversations about a dream.
  • Your profile (optional): details you choose to add, such as name, date of birth, gender, relationship status, birthplace, current location, a short biography and a photo.
  • Google sign-in (only if it is available and you use it): Google's account identifier for you and the email address Google verifies. We do not receive your Google password.

Everything in your journal is tied to your account and kept separate from other people's. Loominous does not show your dreams to other users.

Loominous uses these services to run. They receive only what the feature needs.

Each of these providers has its own policies for how long it keeps data. We do not use your dreams to advertise to you or sell your data.

Cookies, tracking and analytics

Loominous does not use advertising or analytics trackers. To keep you signed in, the app saves a sign-in token and your username in your browser's local storage on your device. Signing out removes them. If you use Google sign-in, a short-lived technical cookie protects that sign-in attempt and expires within minutes.

Email

We use your email only to verify your account and to send a password-reset link when you ask for one.

Your choices and control

  • Download: Settings → Account → Download my dreams gives you a text file of your dreams, built on your own device.
  • Delete a dream: removes it and asks Pinecone to remove its embedding.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete my account permanently removes your dreams, readings, personal meanings, feedback, profile, sign-in links and login, and asks Pinecone to remove your embeddings. This cannot be undone.

We may keep encrypted backups of the database, which can still contain deleted data until those backups are replaced. Service providers listed above may keep their own copies for their own retention periods.

Security

Passwords are hashed with a random salt. Connections to Loominous use HTTPS. Sign-in links and tokens expire. No online service can promise perfect security, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

Loominous is not designed for young children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Loominous handles data, we will update this page and its date.
