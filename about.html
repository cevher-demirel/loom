Loominous

Loominous is a private dream journal. Write down a dream and Loominous offers a reading, then weaves your dreams together on a Loom Map so you can see what keeps returning.

What you can do

  • Record dreams: write each dream in your own words and get an AI reading to reflect on.
  • See patterns on the Loom Map: Symbols, Emotions, People and Places from all your dreams are kept separate, so you can follow each thread.
  • Find related dreams: Loominous connects dreams that echo one another.
  • Keep control: download your dreams or delete your account whenever you like.

Your dreams stay yours

Your journal is private to your account. We do not sell your dreams, use them for advertising, or install advertising trackers. The Privacy Policy explains which services process your data and how deletion works.
